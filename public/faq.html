Ground Rules

Rules & Regulations

⏰
Arrive Early

Please reach the venue at least 10 minutes before your slot begins so play can start on time.

💳
Advance Payment

Your booking is confirmed only after the advance payment is successfully completed. Unpaid slots remain open.

🚭
No Smoking or Alcohol

Smoking, alcohol, and any form of intoxication are strictly prohibited on the premises at all times.

🧹
Keep It Clean

Help us maintain a clean and hygienic environment. Dispose of waste in designated bins.

🕒
Respect Slot Timing

Please vacate the ground promptly when your booked duration ends. Overtime may affect other players.

🤝
Respect Others

Misconduct, abusive language, or unsporting behaviour toward fellow players or staff will not be tolerated.

🎥
CCTV Security

The entire premises are monitored via CCTV round the clock for the safety and security of all players.

🌧️
Weather Policy

In cases of heavy rain or adverse weather, management reserves the right to reschedule or cancel a slot.

🔒
Personal Belongings

Management is not responsible for lost or stolen items. Please keep your valuables secure at all times.

Policy AcknowledgementBy confirming a booking with Royal Turf, you acknowledge that you have read and agreed to the above rules and regulations. We appreciate your cooperation in keeping the venue a safe and enjoyable space for everyone.
